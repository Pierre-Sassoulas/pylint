To do now

Ordered by what unblocks a release or a user first: merges cost one click and ship fixes, reviews of green pull requests come next, then runs worth releasing, then decisions. Inside each step the order is the lanes' ranking: crash or performance, bug, false positive, false negative, enhancement; then High priority; then impact: reactions on the open linked issues plus the net messages the primer says a fix removes from real projects (adds, for a false negative), one point each, leaving out items that show up in three or more pull requests' primer comments, which are noise. A held run is only listed for a crash, a bug, High priority or two reactions. Pull requests waiting on their author are left out until the author has been silent for 30 days (or the PR is labelled Needs take over): then they need you again and come back right after the merges.
